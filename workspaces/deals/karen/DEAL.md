to her, a **35%** profit slice (against the 25% offered), and the fixed-cost risk moved onto
Jamie's side via a *cessão de exploração*. Jamie's revised position held three

and not a business sub-lease — Karen keeps the lease, Jamie manages for a share.

upside is double-dipping. That argument was sent and never answered.
