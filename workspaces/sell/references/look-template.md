# The free look — the template

*Layer-3 reference for [`02_look`](../stages/02_look/CONTEXT.md). One page, four sections,
at most 400 words, in the client's own words and register
([positioning.md](../../../_system/knowledge/positioning.md) § Three registers). It is
free, it follows the first call, and it contains no number — the price is a written thing
that comes after, in the proposal.*

## The sort vocabulary

Every part of the work the look names is sorted with exactly one of four words. They are
the whole method, said plainly:

| Word | Means | Reads to the client as |
|---|---|---|
| **could run itself** | software can do it end to end, today, with what exists | "this is the part I'd build" |
| **keep a person on it** | judgment, relationships, taste — automating it would make it worse | "this stays yours, and should" |
| **worth an assistant** | a person with a tool that does the repetitive half — the sweet spot | "you'd still do it, in a quarter of the time" |
| **don't build this** | not a problem, not yet a problem, or cheaper done by hand for years | "leave it — here's why" |

## The template

```markdown
# <Their business> — what I saw

<One sentence: thank you for the call, here's the look I promised, it's free and it's
mine to be wrong about.>

## What I saw

<Their situation, in their words from the call — the business, the thing that eats the
week, what done looks like to them. Three to five sentences. This is why the page feels
listened-to.>

## Where the work belongs

- **<part of the work>** — could run itself · keep a person on it · worth an assistant ·
  don't build this — <one clause why>
- **<part>** — <word> — <why>
- **<part>** — <word> — <why>
(Every part they named, each with one of the four words. Nothing unsorted.)

## Three things I'd do first

1. <the smallest thing that changes their week — an outcome, not a feature>
2. <the second>
3. <the third>

## How I'd start

<The honest next step, in one short paragraph: "a fixed-price quote for the first of those
three" · "a short paid diagnostic first, because <the system is spec-shaped / the
processes contradict each other> — credited against the build" · "nothing yet — do <X>
by hand for six months and call me when <Y>". Then the one line: "If that sounds right,
I'll put a written proposal together — with numbers — by <day>.">

<One open question, if a [BLOCKER] survived the call — asked, never guessed.>
```

## Rules

- **Their words, their register.** A café hears *bookings* and *the page people find you
  on*; an SME hears *your process* and *your team's week*; no one hears *pipeline*.
- **No number.** Not the floor, not a band, not "roughly". The page earns the proposal.
- **Under 400 words.** A look they read beats a thorough one they don't.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** "I won't know until I see <X>" is house style.
- **It is an edit surface.** Whatever Jamie leaves is what he sends and what `03_quote`
  scopes from.
