# Casey Hebbel — quote (scope · shape · number · deviations)

*Summary of record. The canonical document is
`projects/casey-hebbel/.icm/docs/quote-casey-2026-08-19.md`; the styled version she
received is served at `/quote` in the client repo (password-gated). Prepared
2026-08-19, valid to 2026-09-19.*

## Shape
Fixed-price, two packages, 50% on signature / 50% on launch. Two revision rounds per
sign-off point. She owns brand, content, domain and data outright on final payment;
**code is licensed, not transferred** — handover to another developer quoted
separately. Monthly running cost €0 except ~€14/yr domain on her own card.

## The number
- **Package A — "Opening Night": €1,200** (€1,476 inc. PT VAT 23%). Brand foundation
  (logo + identity system), one deliberately-built page, domain + hosting + `casey@`
  email, enquiry form + mailing list, legal pages (privacy, terms, cancellation,
  under-18 consent), Google Business Profile + local listings + technical SEO,
  analytics with a written readout one month post-launch, manual payment link.
- **Package B — "Opening Night + Box Office": €2,400** (€2,952 inc. VAT). A plus
  self-serve 1:1 booking, weekly class enrolment with capacity + waitlist, Stripe
  checkout.

## Deviations
- Package B is above her stated ceiling ("under €1,500") — offered knowingly as the
  honest price of the self-serve half; Jamie expects her to take A.
- Her full wish-list was framed in the quote as a €5,000–7,000 project; the two
  packages are the deliberate split down to her budget.
- The quote answers the three questions she deferred to Jamie (who updates the site →
  Jamie per job; support shape → no retainer proposed; search → free half only).
- The quote also asks her for a **business name** — a question that exists nowhere
  else, and which everything visual is downstream of.
