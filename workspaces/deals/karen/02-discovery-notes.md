**Leverage note (carry into the proposal's fairness pitch).** Karen/the owners **draw a salary
but will not work** during Jamie's operating period (questionnaire §C):
- That salary is **remuneration for no operating work** → it should be **excluded / added
  back in the Net Profit definition** (template Clause 2(a)), raising the pool Jamie shares.
- With the counterparties stepping back and Jamie supplying effectively all the operating
  labour, the **>50% profit-share ask is credible**.

**Protection focus for a no-buyout phase.** With no equity, no buyout **and no Base/floor**
(Jamie is paid solely by the 75% share — user-confirmed), Jamie's downside protection rests on:
(1) a clear, manipulation-proof **Net Profit definition** + audit/access rights, with the
**owner-salary add-back** (Karen + partner) raising the pool; (2) **control of part-time labour**
— Jamie flexes staff hours/cost down through the lean winter (the lever that replaces a floor);
and (3) **without-cause termination** terms that pay out **accrued profit-share on exit**
(Clause 27). These replace both the buyout mechanics and a guaranteed Base as the load-bearing
terms this phase.
