Olá Diogo, Rita! A few precise questions so the booking calendar and prices match
exactly how you really operate — fast answers here mean fewer wrong bookings later.

**1. Big groups.** You told me max capacity is 14 guests by combining vehicles — but the
T3 takes 8 and the small classics 3 each, so 14 needs *three* cars, and you also said
you have **2 drivers**. What's the real rule for big groups? (Who drives the third car —
or is the practical max 11? Should the site simply say "for groups above 8, contact
us"?)

**2. Sharing a car.** Can two separate bookings ever share the same car or departure
(strangers together, paying per seat)? Or is every booking always private — the party
gets the car(s) to themselves? This decides how the calendar counts seats.

**3. Weddings deposit.** The 30% deposit that holds the date — it's non-refundable
inside a window before the event. How long is that window? (For example: non-refundable
inside 30 days of the event; free date-change before that.) And is there a minimum quote
value? *(Until you answer, the site uses sensible defaults: 30 days.)*

**4. Instagram & Facebook access.** For the automatic posting we'll need admin access to
the Facebook Page and the Instagram Business account (they must be linked to each
other). Is the Instagram account (agorasim.pt) a Business/Creator account, and who
admins the Facebook Page? Meta's approval takes weeks, so I want to start it now.

**5. Written, for the privacy policy and the terms page** (the site cannot lose its
draft banners without these): RNAAT registration number · insurance provider + policy
number · whether you invoice customers yourselves. And one sign-off: we keep
unconverted enquiries for **24 months** before anonymising them — OK, or name a
different number.

**6. The domain.** Any news from the previous provider on recovering agorasim.pt? The
site switches to the real domain (and live payments) the moment we have it.

**7. "PAX" in your price table — do children count?** Example: 2 adults + 6 children
on a private Countryside tour. Is that the **8 PAX** row (500€), or priced as 2 adults
(220€) + 6 children (6×30€ = 180€ → 400€)? Same question for minimums: can 1 adult +
1 child book the public Óbidos tour (min. 2 PAX)? This decides real money at checkout,
so I need the rule exactly as you charge it.

**8. Óbidos departure times.** The Countryside tour is 10h00/14h00 — what are the
Óbidos departures? If the exact time depends on the day, the site will say "hora exata
confirmada por email" — just tell me which way you want it.

**9. Manzwine minimum.** Your table says "min 2 PAX" — is that 2 *adults*, or do
children count? (Right now a parent + child can't add Manzwine; tell me if that's
wrong.)

**10. Cancellations.** When a guest cancels online more than 48h before (free
cancellation), should the refund go out **automatically**, or do you want to confirm
each one first from the admin? Automatic is smoother for guests; confirming gives you
the last word.

**11. Photos, one more time.** The WeTransfer links from August have expired. Could
you re-send — especially the **Renault 4L and VW T3 wedding photos** (the weddings
page still says "photographs on their way" for those two)? WhatsApp as documents, or

**12. Two small checks.** The "Wedding Awards 2022–2026" badges — where are those
