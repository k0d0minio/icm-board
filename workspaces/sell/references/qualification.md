# Qualification — take, decline, or not-yet

*Layer-3 reference for [`01_intake`](../stages/01_intake/CONTEXT.md). A verdict cites one
of these criteria by ID; "seems nice" is not a criterion. Calibrated by
[`target-profile.md`](target-profile.md) and [`services.md`](../../../_system/knowledge/services.md);
sharpened by ICM-009's questionnaire answers and by every decline that later proves wrong.*

**The intake form answers are the first evidence.** A lead who arrived through the
portfolio's `/start` form has already said what the business does, what eats the week,
which tools, what done would look like, and a budget band — the dashboard snapshots those
into the engagement's `answers/intake-diagnostic.md`. Read them before the row's message;
cite them beside the criterion they answer.

## Take — all four hold

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Q1 | The ask is a service we sell (or one deliberate step from it) — `services.md` says so |
| Q2 | They can plausibly clear the pricing floor, or the in-kind value is real and wanted |
| Q3 | A named person can say yes — we are not pitching into a committee fog |
| Q4 | Nothing in it crosses a red line in [`terms.md`](../../../_system/knowledge/terms.md) |

## Decline — any one suffices

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| D1 | It matches `services.md` § Non-services |
| D2 | Below floor with no honest smaller version they'd accept |
| D3 | The relationship already shows the worst-client pattern in `target-profile.md` |
| D4 | We'd be the third contractor on the same haunted project — the haunting is the product |

## Not-yet — park, with a date

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| N1 | Right ask, wrong moment (their funding, our capacity) — `DEAL.md` records the revisit date |
| N2 | A `[BLOCKER]`-grade unknown that one conversation would resolve — go get the conversation |

A decline is still sent kindly and, where honest, points somewhere better — the estate
runs on referrals and a good decline is marketing.
