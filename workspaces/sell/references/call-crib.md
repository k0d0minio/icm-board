# The first call — the crib

*Layer-3 reference for [`02_look`](../stages/02_look/CONTEXT.md). Twenty to thirty minutes,
four beats, no number in the room. Distilled 2026-09-22 from
[`discovery-interview.md`](discovery-interview.md) § Before · The craft · The arc (the
first four beats) — that file keeps the diagnostic's full arc; this one is the free look's
call. A crib sheet for a conversation, not a form to read aloud.*

## Before — five minutes

Re-read `01-intake.md`, the `intake-diagnostic` answers if they filled the form, and
anything they sent or linked. Look at their public presence as a customer would. Never
ask what a document already answers. Pick, by ID from
[`discovery-questions.md`](discovery-questions.md), the two or three `[BLOCKER]`s the
look cannot be written without — those are the whole question list.

## The craft

- **Rounds of at most three questions.** If an answer opens a more important thread,
  follow it and come back.
- **Interrogate vague answers.** "Modern", "easy", "like theirs" are starting points: what
  specifically, show me, what would you hate.
- **Accept "I don't know."** Say what you'd assume and why, get a nod, and write it down as
  an *assumption*, flagged — never as a decision.
- **Replay before leaving**: their story in two sentences, the job, what done looks like,
  the blockers. Let them correct it.

## The arc — four beats

1. **Their story.** "Tell me about the business, then about the thing that made you
   write." Do not interrupt with solutions. The look will quote them.
2. **The job to be done.** What must change for this to have been worth it? What happens
   today instead — the manual process, the workaround, the thing that eats the week?
3. **Done looks like.** The single sentence they'd accept as "shipped". Push past features
   to outcomes.
4. **The two or three `[BLOCKER]`s** from the prep — the questions a responsible look
   cannot be written without. Two that hide: who decides (B4) and what already exists and
   who controls it (S4).

## No number in the room

The look is free and says so; the price is a written thing, after the look. If they ask:
"I'll put it in writing once I've had a proper look — the page comes first, and it's free."
The one exception is the floor conversation, when the ask is plainly below it
([qualification.md](qualification.md) D2) — said kindly, as a fact about how the work is
priced, not a negotiation.

## The closing ask

- Links: the site, the tools they use, anything they'd want you to see.
- **A screen recording** of the thing that eats the week — five minutes of them doing it,
  narrated. It is the best input a look can have. Voice notes are fine too; both are
  transcribed locally and never uploaded ([stack.md](../../../_system/knowledge/stack.md)
  § Local tools).
- Read-only access to a back office, **only if they offer it**, via the password manager.
- "You'll have a page from me by <day>. Free, no strings; it'll say where I'd start."

## After — the same day

Notes or the transcript into the engagement's `raw/` while they are warm; anything
promised in the room at the top of the look's draft so it cannot evaporate.
