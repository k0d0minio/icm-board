## 1. The deal in one line
Jamie operates Karen's leased business full-time as GM, invoicing (recibos verdes) for a
**monthly share of a defined Net Profit** — paid **solely by the 75% share, no Base/floor** —
with **full control of part-time staff within a budget at Karen's cost**, on an **initial trial
term** — **no buyout** this phase. Counterparty = **Karen**.

## 2. Route — confirmed
- **Invoice / recibos verdes.** No employment relationship; Jamie carries own IVA/IRS/
  Segurança Social. Simple, right for a trial.
- **Buyout out of scope.** Not part of this deal. If ever revisited after the trial, the
  **target** would need resolving — assignment of **Karen's lease** (*cessão de exploração*)
  vs purchase of **Afonso's quotas** (different counterparty/mechanics). Flag only.

## 3. The central term — Net Profit definition (this is where money is won or lost)
The profit-share % is meaningless until "Net Profit" is pinned down. Recommended basis:

- **Net Profit = Gross Profit − agreed Operating Expenses** (a fixed Schedule-1 line-item
  list), **before IRC and before Jamie's own fee.** Rationale: tax and financing choices
  shouldn't move Jamie's share, and a pre-tax operating figure is the least gameable.
  *(Confirm IRC treatment with the TOC — if Karen's operation genuinely bears IRC as a cost,
  decide explicitly whether it sits above or below the share line; default above = excluded.)*
- **Add back (do NOT let these reduce the pool):**
  - **Karen's/owners' salary or drawings** — they won't work, so this is owner remuneration,
    not an operating cost (template Clause 2(a)). This is a concrete, fair argument.
  - **Related-party costs above arm's length** (Clause 2(b)) — esp. anything routed via
    Afonso beyond the fixed €2,500 rent.
  - **Capex and one-off / non-recurring items** (Clause 2(c)/(d)) — agree D&A treatment
    (the P&L's €100/mo is trivial; simplest to exclude D&A and handle capex separately).
- **Non-negotiable companion: audit & access rights** (Clause 9) — full access to POS/Z-
  reports, bank, invoices on notice. Without this, every number is unverifiable. This *is*
  the protection in a no-equity deal.
- **Payment mechanics (Clause 7, adapted — added 2026-07):** the monthly figure is prepared
  off an agreed one-page template (one party prepares, the other checks), paid by the **15th
  of the following month**; a **loss carries forward** against future profit before anything
  is shared (no annual true-up needed on a 3-month trial — carry-forward does the same job);
  the **TOC's figure settles disputes** (Clause 19, light form). Also settle before day one:
  **which entity Jamie invoices** (Karen vs BARZA — IVA/bookkeeping, ask the TOC) and the
  practical **cash/banking/supplier-payment setup** within the budget.
- **Cash-vs-pool note (for the worked example, Schedule 2):** because the owner salaries are
  paid in cash but added back, Karen's total take (salary + 25% of pool) equals **25% of the
  pool** — her salary is effectively an advance on her share. That is the intended logic of
  §2 of the proposal, but it must be made visible in the worked example before signing; if
  the salaries exceed 25% of the pool in a lean month, Karen funds the difference. Surface
  it with numbers, not clause-language, and expect it to be the negotiation flashpoint.

## 4. Profit-share % — stance
- **75% to Jamie, 25% to Karen** (user decision). Justification: Jamie supplies effectively
  **all** operating labour and management; Karen/owners step back to a passive return on a
  business they're not running. Karen's 25% is her return for holding the lease and the asset
  risk (rent commitment, the 8-yr *cessão*).
- The majority ask is credible precisely because of the salary add-back (§3) — frame it as
  "I run it, so I take the operator's majority; you keep a passive owner's share."
- **Order of operations:** simple — there's **no Base**, so the 75% applies straight to the
  defined Net Profit each month (worked example in template Schedule 2 for clarity). Jamie is
  paid **only** by the share.

## 5. No Base / floor — stance (user decision)
- **The user has removed the Base.** Jamie is paid **solely by the 75% share** — no separate
  fee, no guaranteed monthly floor. In a quiet month the share is small or nil, and Jamie
  carries that.
- **What protects the downside instead** (the levers that replace a floor):
  1. **The owner-salary add-back** (§3) — Karen + partner draw salary but stay hands-off, so it
     adds back into the pool, structurally lifting Jamie's share.
  2. **Control of part-time labour** — Jamie hires/fires/schedules the part-time team within an
     agreed monthly budget (at Karen's cost), so he can **flex the biggest variable cost down**
     through the Jan–Mar trough rather than being trapped by it.
  3. **Without-cause termination payout** (§7) — accrued-but-unpaid share paid out on exit.
- Net effect: the trough risk is **managed operationally** (flex cost, lean overhead) rather
  than insured by a fixed floor. This is the user's chosen risk posture.

## 6. Authority limit, staff control & reserved matters
- Jamie has **full operational + budget control** (confirmed role) — the authority limit must
  be high enough to actually run P&L, or the profit-share is hollow. Recommend: **commit
  routine opex/purchasing within the operating budget without prior approval; per-item cap
  (illustrative €750–1,000) above which Karen's sign-off is needed.**
- **Staff (user decision):** Jamie **hires, fires and schedules the part-time staff at his own
  discretion within an agreed monthly budget, wages carried by the business (Karen's cost)**.
  **BARZA remains the sole legal employer**, carries payroll/TSU/severance, and **indemnifies
  Jamie** against employment claims. The two full-timers are Karen + partner (owners), outside
  this — they keep their salaries and stay hands-off.
- **Reserved to Karen:** new debt, capex above a threshold, changing the concept, the lease,
  related-party deals, anything touching Afonso's headlease. *(Staff hire/fire is no longer
  reserved — it sits with Jamie within the agreed budget.)*

## 7. Term & protection (the load-bearing terms this phase)
- **Initial trial term** — a fixed **3 months** (user decision), with a written **review
  point**, then rolling month-to-month or a longer fixed term if both want to continue. This
  *is* the "test the place" period.
- **Without-cause termination:** notice period + **payout of accrued-but-unpaid profit-share
  to the exit date** (Clause 27). With **no equity and no Base**, this is the main thing
  protecting Jamie's unpaid build-up — make it explicit and non-trivial.
- **Confidentiality / no key-staff poaching** as appropriate (light touch for a trial).

## 8. The three priorities, mapped to this phase
1. **Net Profit definition + exclusions** → §3 (the salary add-back is the live win).
2. **T12-NP valuation basis** → **not in scope** (buyout out of scope); would only resurface
   for any future buyout.
3. **Invoice-route downside protection** → §5 (owner-salary add-back + part-time cost-flex,
   in place of a Base) + §7 without-cause payout (these are the protection now, in place of
   both buyout mechanics and a floor).

3. Confirm the **owner (Karen + partner) salary quantum** — the add-back figure.
4. Confirm **operating entity** Karen uses + lease permits Jamie to operate (sub-delegation).
5. **2024–25 P&Ls stand as the baseline** (user accepts no fresh monthly figures) — confirm
