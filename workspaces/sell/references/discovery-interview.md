# Discovery interview — the script

*Layer-3 reference for [`02_discovery`](../stages/02_discovery/CONTEXT.md). A crib sheet
for a conversation, not a form to read aloud. Skeleton distilled from the shape of the
estate's real discovery work (berceo, messy-play); ICM-006 tracks folding those actual
artifacts in from a local session.*

## Before — five minutes of prep

Re-read `01-intake.md`. Write the prep doc (`02-discovery-prep.md`) with the short list:
**the questions to ask if we only get one meeting**, chosen by ID from
[`discovery-questions.md`](discovery-questions.md), `[BLOCKER]`s first.

## The arc — 30–45 minutes

1. **Their story first.** "Tell me about the business, then about the thing you want
   built." Do not interrupt with solutions. The job here is their words — the register,
   the proposal and half the tickets will quote them.
2. **The job to be done.** What must this thing do for it to have been worth paying for?
   What happens today instead — the manual process, the workaround, the lost customer?
3. **Done looks like.** The single sentence they'd accept as "shipped". Push past
   features to outcomes: fewer no-shows? found on Google? bookings without phone calls?
4. **The short list.** Work through the prep doc's `[BLOCKER]`s. These are the questions
   a responsible quote cannot be written without — do not leave without them.
5. **Constraints, lightly.** Deadline and why · budget shape (a range signal, not a
   negotiation) · content and materials — who writes, who photographs · anything
   `[LAWYER]` (their sector's rules, data they hold).
6. **What happens next, said plainly.** "I'll send a written proposal by <day>. It will
   have a number in it." No number in the room unless it is the floor conversation.

## After — same day

Raw notes into the deal folder while they're warm; the stage turns them into
`02-discovery-notes.md`. Anything promised in the room ("I'll send you X") goes in the
notes' top section so it cannot evaporate.
