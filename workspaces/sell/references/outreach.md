# Outreach — the outbound playbook

*Layer-3 reference for [`01_intake`](../stages/01_intake/CONTEXT.md) when the deal is one
we started. Messages are drafted in the deal folder and **sent by Jamie** — no outbound
action from a session, ever. Voice per
[`voice.md`](../../../_system/knowledge/voice.md); shapes seeded from
[`_system/setup/questionnaire.md`](../../../_system/setup/questionnaire.md) § Outreach (ICM-009).*

## The posture

- **Specific beats scaled.** One message that proves we looked at *their* thing beats
  fifty from a template. Outbound here is a craft channel, not a volume channel.
- **Offer a observation, not a pitch.** The first message gives something true and
  useful about their situation; the ask is a conversation.
- **One follow-up, then silence.** Two unanswered messages is the answer. The prospect
  goes back to the pool with a note, not onto a drip.
- **Track it like inbound.** The moment they reply, a Neon row exists and the ladder
  applies — outreach is just another way to arrive at `new`.

## Message shapes

Each shape gets a real example once ICM-009 lands; until then the slots are honest gaps.

### Cold — first contact
— not yet established. Shape: one observed specific about them → one relevant thing we
did → one-line ask for a conversation. No attachment, no price, under 120 words.

### Warm — referral or prior contact
— not yet established. Shape: the shared point of contact up front, then as cold.

### Follow-up — the only one
— not yet established. Shape: shorter than the first; adds one new specific; makes
silence easy to end either way.

## The pool

Prospects not yet contacted live nowhere special — a deal folder is only opened when a
message is actually drafted. A candidate list, when one exists, is a note in
[`../../deals/README.md`](../../deals/README.md)'s terms: not a deal, not a row, just a
line in `.icm/docs/` here if it needs writing down at all.
