# Vinecliff — the sit-down with Billy (discovery prep)

*The question set for one working session with Billy Carlson. The platform at
vinecliff.vercel.app is built; these answers are what separate a demo from his
business. Ordered so the money questions land while energy is high. Bring the admin
open on a phone — several answers can be typed straight in during the meeting.*

## 1. The offer — what does each space actually rent for?
The site currently shows **invented placeholder rates**. For each of Farmhouse,
Carriage House, Barn, Whole Estate:
- Nightly rate, weekly rate, cleaning fee — and do rates change by season?
- Event-day pricing for the Barn and Whole Estate (the site guesses $2,500 + $500 and
  $3,500/day · $20,000/wk · $1,000).
- Minimum stay per space? (weekend minimum? week-only in summer?)
- What's included (linens, firewood, cleaning) vs extra?

## 2. Booking rules — how does he actually operate?
- How far ahead can people book, and how last-minute is too last-minute?
- Turnover buffer between stays (the site assumes buffer days — how many)?
- Party caps per building; when does an event "take over" the estate? (The engine
  models the barn/whole-estate blocking everything else — is that right?)
- Weddings/reunions: max guests, noise/curfew rules, anything he refuses.

## 3. Money
- Does he have (or want) a **Stripe account** to take cards, or does he prefer
  invoice/check/deposit-by-hand to start?
- Deposit policy: how much holds a date, when is the balance due, what's refundable?
- Cancellation policy he actually honours.
- Who invoices, and does anything need to appear for tax purposes?

## 4. Calendar & channels
- Where do bookings live today (paper, Google Calendar, Airbnb, VRBO)?
- Anything already booked for the coming season that must be entered as blackouts?
- Does he want the iCal sync to Airbnb/VRBO/Google switched on (already built)?

## 5. Content & identity
- The **ten videos** he supplied — where does he want them (hero, per-space pages,
  gallery)?
- Real photos per space, and of the grounds? (Site currently leans on a few.)
- House rules text per building; anything to correct on the History page?
- Domain: does he own vinecliff.com (or another)? Email: what address should
  confirmations come from — and does `stay@`/`hello@` need setting up?

## 6. Operations
- Who answers enquiries and approves bookings — Billy alone? Anyone else needing an
  admin account?
- Notifications: where should new-booking/enquiry alerts land (email, phone)?
- 15 minutes: walk him through the admin (approve, decline, block dates, enter a phone
  booking) — his reaction is discovery data.

## 7. The engagement itself
- What does he actually want from Jamie: a finished site handed over (**fixed-price
  build**), or Jamie running it and improving it (**retainer**)? Present both shapes,
  with a number range for each, and watch which one he leans into.
- When would he want to be live for real bookings — this season or next?
- What would make this a clear yes for him — and what's the one thing he's worried
  about?

## 8. Close
- Anything I haven't asked that matters to how Vinecliff runs?
- Agree the next concrete step before leaving the table (rates delivered by X, a yes
  on shape by Y).
