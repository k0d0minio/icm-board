# Target profile — who outbound goes looking for

*Layer-3 reference for [`01_intake`](../stages/01_intake/CONTEXT.md) (fit check) and the
[`outreach`](outreach.md) playbook (who to approach). Filled 2026-08-26 from
[`_system/setup/questionnaire.md`](../../../_system/setup/questionnaire.md) § Target
profile (ICM-009) — from Jamie's real best and worst clients, not an imagined persona.*

## The good-client pattern

What the best clients had in common **before the first invoice** — the more of these a
lead shows, the harder to say no:

- **Came by referral** — arrived pre-sold through someone who vouched; trust existed
  before the first call.
- **Own a real operating business** — customers today; the build serves something that
  already works.
- **One person who decides fast and pays fast** — quick yes, deposit without chasing.
- **Brought their content** — words, photos and opinions ready; the build never stalled
  on materials.

## The worst-client pattern

The signals qualification (D3 in [`qualification.md`](qualification.md)) catches at
intake, while there is still time to say no:

- **Haggling before scoping** — price pressure before the work is even understood; the
  below-scope red line ([terms.md](../../../_system/knowledge/terms.md)) arriving early.
- **Idea-stage, no business** — a venture that exists only as the thing we'd build; no
  customers, no revenue, high drama.
- **Vague unlimited vision** — "we'll know it when we see it"; no stable done-looks-like
  sentence survives discovery.

## Who we want more of

**Both lines, weighted to AI.** Local operating businesses (Mafra/Lisboa cafés,
restaurants, venues, practices) keep the referral engine and the site work warm — they
are the core, not the growth bet. The growth bet outbound actually chases is **SMEs with
a real process worth automating** — the AI consulting / agent-workflow line: bigger
engagements, retainer-friendly, remote-friendly.

## Where they are

- **Today's real channels** — referrals and word of mouth, plus the in-person Mafra
  network. Both feed the local line and should be tended, not replaced.
- **The AI-SME channel** — *not yet established.* Where those companies actually get
  found (LinkedIn, the portfolio, speaking, partner referrals) is the open question the
  outreach playbook's first outbound deals should answer empirically.
