# Services — what is actually sold

*Layer-3 reference. The catalogue every quote and proposal draws from — a deal is one or
more of these, scoped. Filled from [`../setup/questionnaire.md`](../setup/questionnaire.md) § Services (ICM-009);
until then the structure is real and the content is an honest gap.*

## The catalogue

One entry per thing Jamie sells, in the shape below. If a piece of work fits no entry,
that is a finding — either the catalogue grows, or the work is declined.

```markdown
### <Service name>
- **What the client gets** — the deliverable in their words, not the stack's.
- **Typical shape** — fixed-price build · retainer · in-kind (per pricing.md).
- **In scope** — what is always included.
- **Not in scope** — what is always extra, so quotes never re-litigate it.
- **Smallest honest version** — the least that can be sold without embarrassment.
```
